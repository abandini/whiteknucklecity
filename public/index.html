How We Earned The Name

A partial, non-exhaustive field guide to the moments that taught Cleveland to clench.

1981
Red Right 88
Frozen Municipal Stadium, playoffs. With a field goal to win it, the Browns threw instead. Interception in the end zone. Season over.
1987
The Drive
98 yards, 5:32 on the clock, the AFC Championship in hand. Elway marched the length of the field and took it to Denver.
We had it. That's usually the problem.
1988
The Fumble
Earnest Byner, eight yards from tying the AFC title game, loses it on the goal line. Two years running against the same team.
1989
The Shot
Michael Jordan over Craig Ehlo at the buzzer, Game 5. The freeze-frame of him hanging in the air is required footage in every Cleveland sports documentary.
1995
The Move
Art Modell announced the Browns were leaving for Baltimore — mid-season, with home games still left to play. The city kept the name, the colors, and the records. Football came back in 1999. Trust took longer.
They took the team. Cleveland kept the grip.
1997
Two Outs In The Ninth
The Indians were one out from a World Series title in Game 7. The lead didn't survive the bottom of the ninth. The Marlins won it in extras.
Three outs from heaven. None of them came.
2010
The Decision
"I'm going to take my talents to South Beach." A prime-time television special. Some jerseys did not survive the night.
2016 · The Release
Cavs. Champions. 3–1.
Down 3–1 to a 73-win Warriors team. The Block, the Shot, and 52 years of drought gone in a single night. For one summer, the knuckles went pink again.
2016
…And Right Back Into It
Four months later the Indians took a 3–1 World Series lead, then Game 7, extra innings, a rain delay for effect. Cubs win.
The only city that can win 3–1 and lose 3–1 in the same year.
2017
0–16
The Browns finished a perfect winless season. Fans held a parade for it. You have to grip something even when there's nothing left to hold.

The Short Version

What is White Knuckle City?

A nickname for Cleveland, Ohio — shorthand for what it feels like to root for its teams. The white knuckles come standard with the Guardians, Browns, and Cavaliers.

Why is Cleveland called White Knuckle City?

Decades of tense, late, heartbreaking finishes — Red Right 88, The Drive, The Fumble, The Shot, The Move, Game 7 losses. Even the wins tend to arrive the hard way, so the tension holds until the final out.

Which teams make Cleveland a white knuckle city?

The Cleveland Guardians (MLB), the Cleveland Browns (NFL), and the Cleveland Cavaliers (NBA).

What is the White Knuckle Index?

A live tension meter on this site. It reads the current Cleveland scores and rates the moment from 0 (relaxed) to 100 (maximum grip).
